Module 3: Performance Evaluation and Improvement (Clauses 9 and 10)

Factory scenario: 24/7 production with quarterly compliance sampling, high scrap variability, and recurring odor complaints.

Real Audit Scenario: Monitoring Data Integrity Breakdown

Observed: Emission readings in monthly reports do not match raw instrument exports; calibration overdue by two months.

Audit test: Trace data from instrument to KPI dashboard, verify calibration certificates, and inspect correction rules for invalid data points.

Expected evidence: Monitoring plan, calibrated equipment logs, retained raw datasets, and approved trend analysis records.

Practical Examples

  • Clause 9.1.1: Noise, VOC, and wastewater indicators have defined method, frequency, responsibility, and acceptance criteria.
  • Clause 9.1.2: Compliance evaluation schedule aligns with permit deadlines and legal register revision cycles.
  • Clause 9.2: Internal audit uses process-based sampling, including maintenance shutdown and startup transitions.
  • Clause 10.2: Corrective actions include root cause analysis and effectiveness checks after 60 days.

Failure Cases & Audit Signals

  • Internal audits repeat checklist questions but miss high-risk process changes.
  • Corrective actions close on paperwork while recurrence continues on the shop floor.
  • Management review receives lagging KPIs only, without risk projection or resource decisions.

Major NC Risk — Escalate Immediately

  • Performance data is unreliable or manipulated, preventing valid compliance determination (Clause 9.1.1).
  • Organization cannot demonstrate periodic legal compliance evaluation despite applicable obligations (Clause 9.1.2).
  • Systemic nonconformities recur without effective corrective action, indicating EMS failure (Clause 10.2).
